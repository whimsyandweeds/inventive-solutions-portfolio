Survivor Resource Directory

Independent websites and projects started by survivors themselves — memorials, family-court help, stories, money, pets, tech safety, and people trying to connect it all. No big institutions. Just women who lived it and built something.

Reading this somewhere you're monitored? Use the red Quick Exit button at the top at any time. When you visit another site, check whether it has its own quick-exit button before you stay there.

What these sites recommend most

The books, tools, and guides that come up again and again across the independent sites in the directory below — start here if you're in a hurry.

“Why Does He Do That?” — Lundy Bancroft

The book recommended more than any other. Understanding abusive behavior and control. Free with a library card via the Libby app (ebook or audiobook). Also borrowable on Open Library.

Borrow free → Libby

WomensLaw.org

State-by-state legal information for survivors, in plain language.

Visit → womenslaw.org

NNEDV Safety Net — Tech Safety

Guides for checking phones, location sharing, and online accounts for stalking and tracking.

Visit → techsafety.org

myPlan app

A private safety-planning app that helps you weigh risks and plan next steps.

Visit → myplanapp.org

DomesticShelters.org

Searchable help finder plus articles on leaving, pets, and practical planning.

Visit → domesticshelters.org

Aspire News app

A disguised safety app — it looks like a news app and can quietly alert trusted contacts.

Visit → whengeorgiasmiled.org

Tech-safety guides

Coalition Against Stalkerware, Cornell’s CETA clinic, EFF Surveillance Self-Defense, and Apple’s Personal Safety User Guide.

Documentation tools

VictimsVoice, OurFamilyWizard, and Alimentor — for logging incidents and co-parenting communication.

More books survivors pass around

The Gift of Fear · No Visible Bruises · The Body Keeps the Score · When Dad Hurts Mom — search these in Libby with your library card, or check Open Library.

Safety apps

Bright Sky, Noonlight, and bSafe.

RedRover Relief

Help with the cost of boarding pets so you don’t have to leave them behind.

Visit → redrover.org

RAINN

Support and resources focused on sexual assault, recommended often on sexual-assault survivor sites.

Visit → rainn.org
