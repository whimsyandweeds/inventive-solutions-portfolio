Ladies,

The system was used to protect my abuser’s “right” to kick my door in whenever he pleased. I watched it fail over and over: fail to use common sense, and fail to protect the people who needed it most. That’s when I knew we had a HUGE problem.

EVERY WOMAN IS AT RISK. The system waits until you’re in the hospital or dead to take action.

So I’m tracking the REAL data, so we can fight back. They only see one case at a time, so they never see the pattern. But if we come together with the real numbers, our daughters will at least have a chance.

I want y’all’s feedback. Hundreds of heads are better than one, and united we are stronger. Send me any thoughts or suggestions you have 💜

Thank y’all so much for being here.
