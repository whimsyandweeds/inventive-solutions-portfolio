Legal & Systems Abuse · Anonymous survey

Has the system been used against you?

Some abusers don’t stop at home. They use police, courts, and other systems to keep controlling, punishing, or exhausting you — especially when you try to leave. If that happened to you, this short questionnaire helps us track the pattern: how often it happens, which systems are involved, and how.

What this is called

Legal abuse — also called abusive litigation
Using courts and legal processes — endless motions, custody fights, false reports, draining legal costs — as a way to keep harassing and controlling you.
Systems abuse
Turning systems that are supposed to help — police, CPS, housing, immigration, protective orders — into weapons against you.
Institutional betrayal
When the system that should have protected you sides with him, ignores you, or punishes you for asking for help.

Before you start — please read this

Answer only what feels safe to answer.

  • Don’t type names, addresses, case numbers, or anything that could identify you, him, or your kids.
  • Every question is optional. Skip anything you’re not sure about.
  • Leave the contact field blank if someone else checks your phone or email.
  • The Quick Exit at the top stays on screen the whole time.

●This is anonymous unless you choose otherwise. We’re counting patterns — not building a case about any one person.
